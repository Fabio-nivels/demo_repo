Purchase Email Product Documents

The Purchase Email Product Documents module streamlines your purchase order process by allowing you to attach essential product-related documents directly to selected purchase orders. With this module, you can easily preview the attached documents and send them via email to suppliers or stakeholders with just a few clicks. Enhance your communication and ensure all necessary documentation is included, saving time and reducing errors in your procurement workflow.

This module is an ideal tool for businesses looking to maintain professional and efficient purchase order management.

Preview

PO Preview

In preview of the purchase order, you can see the product documents that are available and download them. You can also access PO or RFQ document.

Purchase Order

In RFQ and PO form view, button Preview is available. When you click on it, you will see the preview of the purchase order with product documents depending on the visibility of product document

Settings

In the Setting, you can select all of the Purchase Order reports that you want to add the Product Documents to

Visibility

In Product Documents form, tree, kanban and search view new selection field Visibility on purchase is available. This field has 5 values
